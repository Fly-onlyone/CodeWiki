# The app

The desktop app is where you read the wiki, follow the code and review what CodeWiki proposes.

* [Home](home.md): open a project, or pick up one you opened before.
* [Reading notes](reading-notes.md): the sidebar, the toolbar, tabs, zoom and the details pane.
* [Graph](graph.md): what depends on what.
* [Command palette and shortcuts](command-palette-and-shortcuts.md): everything from the keyboard.
* [Editing and proposals](editing-and-proposals.md): change a note, and what happens to a note you edited.
* [Review](review.md): decide what happens to the changes that wait for you.
* [Themes](themes.md): twelve themes, and your own.
* [Settings](settings.md): appearance, search, backend, repositories, updates, keyboard and diagnostics.
* [Tray and closing](tray.md): closing the window keeps CodeWiki running.
* [Notifications and reconnecting](notifications.md): what the app tells you, and what it does when the backend stops answering.
