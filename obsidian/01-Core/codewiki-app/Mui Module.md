# Mui Module

> Components drawn to Material UI's measurements.

## What it does

The app is native, so Material UI itself cannot be used. Each component here follows the anatomy, sizes and behaviour of its MUI 6 namesake, with the changes the TradingAgent theme makes to it. Colors come from the active CodeWiki theme.

Three things GPUI cannot do shape the result: it cannot scale an element, blur what is behind one, or space letters apart.
